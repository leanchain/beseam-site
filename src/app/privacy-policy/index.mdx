## 2. What data do we collect?

### 2.1 Data you provide

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect:

- your name, work email address, company, role, and contact preferences
- account, subscription, billing, and transaction information
- support requests, messages, feedback, and meeting or review requests
- store domains, product-page URLs, product information, questions, configuration choices, and other content you submit for analysis
- access credentials, tokens, or permissions you choose to provide when connecting a commerce platform or other third-party service

Please do not submit sensitive personal data unless we specifically request it and it is necessary for the Service.

### 2.2 Store, integration, and service data

When you connect a store or use a monitoring or publishing feature, we may process data that you authorize us to access, including:

- catalog, product, variant, price, inventory, storefront, feed, and related commerce data
- approved changes, publishing activity, rollback information, and audit history
- questions, prompts, assistant or search responses, visibility results, findings, recommendations, and reports
- technical information about integrations, synchronization, errors, and service activity

The exact data depends on the features and integrations you enable.

### 2.3 Data collected automatically

When you visit or use the Service, we may collect:

- IP address, browser and device information, operating system, and approximate location derived from IP address
- pages viewed, referring pages, timestamps, interactions, feature usage, and diagnostic events
- security, server, and application logs
- campaign and referral parameters, such as UTM values
- cookie and local-storage choices described in Section 6

### 2.4 Data from public and third-party sources

We may collect information from public storefronts, product pages, feeds, search results, AI-assisted shopping surfaces, and services you connect. We may combine that information with data you provide so we can run checks, identify gaps, and monitor changes.

We use personal data to:

- provide, operate, and support the Service

- measure website and campaign performance when you consent to optional analytics
- secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot errors, and enforce our terms
- improve the reliability, usability, and quality of the Service
- comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and those of others

## 4. What are our legal bases?

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on:

- **Contract:** to provide the Service you request and manage our relationship with you.
- **Legitimate interests:** to operate, secure, support, and improve the Service; understand business use; and communicate with prospective and existing customers.
- **Consent:** for optional analytics, certain integrations, and marketing where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** to keep required records and respond to valid legal requests.

When we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the impact on your rights and do not use this basis where your interests override ours.

## 5. AI-assisted checks and automated processing

The Service may submit questions, public store content, product data, and related technical information to AI, search, or commerce providers to perform a check or deliver a feature. Those providers may process the submitted information under their own terms or under service arrangements with us.

AI-assisted outputs can be incomplete or inaccurate. Beseam does not use these outputs to make decisions about individuals that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Customers remain responsible for reviewing recommendations and approving changes to their stores.

## 6. Cookies, local storage, and analytics

We use essential browser storage and similar technologies to operate and secure the Service and remember choices. For example, this website stores your analytics consent choice in local storage.

Optional Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Hotjar scripts load only after you select **Accept analytics**. If accepted, they may collect page views, interactions, device and connection information, and campaign data to help us understand website performance. Hotjar additionally records anonymised interaction data -- clicks, scrolling, mouse movement and page navigation -- to produce heatmaps and session replays; keystrokes in form fields are masked and we do not use it to identify individuals.

You can reject optional analytics without losing access to the website. You can change your choice by clearing this site's browser storage, which will cause the consent prompt to appear again. You can also use browser controls to block or delete cookies and site data, although some Service features may then work differently.

- **Commerce, AI, search, and integration providers** involved in a feature you request or enable.

- **A successor organization** in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data.
